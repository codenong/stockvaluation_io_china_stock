# Model Selection And Lifecycle

The current local workflow is FCFF-oriented for supported public non-financial operating companies. Model choice and lifecycle are still important, but they mostly guide interpretation and stop conditions.

## When It Matters

- The company is a bank, insurer, broker, asset manager, or other financial firm.
- Debt and reinvestment do not have ordinary operating-company meaning.
- The company is young, high-growth, cyclical, commodity-exposed, declining, distressed, or has negative operating income.
- The service selects a longer template, forced three-stage pattern, or returns model-selection notes.

## Evidence Required

- Company sector and business model.
- Service-returned `primaryModel`, `modelSelectionRationale`, `growthPattern`, `projectionYears`, and `templateSelectionReason`.
- Profitability history, cyclicality, data sufficiency, and management disclosures.

## Allowed action

- Explain FCFF model fit for supported public non-financial companies.
- Stop for financial firms until a governed excess-return, DDM, or FCFE model exists.
- Explain lifecycle effects on growth duration, margin convergence, reinvestment, WACC, and terminal value.
- Lower confidence when lifecycle or data quality makes baseline FCFF fragile.

## Do not

- Do not switch the valuation model autonomously.
- Do not value financial firms with FCFF when the workflow is unsupported.
- Do not normalize distressed, cyclical, or negative earnings by inventing future margins.
- Do not calculate missing valuation values outside MCP/service output.

## Report Guidance

Use lifecycle as a frame for assumptions:

- Young/high-growth: focus on market size, path to margins, and reinvestment.
- Transition: focus on margin convergence and capital efficiency.
- Mature: focus on durable margins, terminal growth, and payout/capital discipline.
- Cyclical/commodity: focus on mid-cycle earnings and data caution.
- Distressed: focus on survival risk and stop if service support is insufficient.

## QA Expectation

Financial firms stop. Other difficult lifecycle cases may receive a report only when the service returns a baseline and the report states model-fit limits plainly.
